    Frequently Asked Questions

    Common Questions About Nirmala Multispeciality Hospital, Rewari

    What are the hospital timings and is emergency available 24x7?

    Yes, Nirmala Multispeciality Hospital operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Our Emergency & Trauma ward, ICU, Pharmacy, Lab, and Ambulance services are available round the clock. OPD timings vary by doctor.

    Is cashless hospitalization / insurance facility available?

    Yes, we provide Cashless Facility for all major TPA (Third Party Administrators) and health insurance companies. Please carry your insurance card/policy at the time of admission for hassle-free processing.

    Which doctors are available and what are their specializations?

    Dr. Jyoti Yadav — MBBS, MS (Obs & Gynae), FMAS — Gynecologist & Obstetrician (Normal/Caesarean Delivery, High-Risk Pregnancy).
    Dr. Ravi Yadav — MBBS, MD, FIDM — Physician, Diabetologist & ICU/Critical Care Specialist.
    Dr. Kavish Yadav — MBBS, MS Ortho — Trauma Specialist & Joint Replacement Surgeon.

    Is ambulance service available? How do I call for an ambulance?

    Yes, 24-hour Ambulance Services are available. You can call our helpline numbers: +91 98969 07555, 971-971-7567, or 9090-9797-59 for immediate ambulance dispatch.

    What diagnostic facilities are available at the hospital?

    We have a fully equipped diagnostic wing with X-Ray, ECG, TMT testing, and a 24x7 Pathology Lab for blood tests and other investigations. Our pharmacy is also open 24x7 for all your medicine needs.

    Where is the hospital located?

    Nirmala Multispeciality Hospital is located at Rajesh Pilot Chowk, Garhi Bolni Road, Rewari, Haryana - 123401. You can easily find us on Google Maps. We are a well-known landmark in Rewari.
